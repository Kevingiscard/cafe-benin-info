Coffee in Benin: a reading guide

A source-first introduction to the Benin dossier, with uncertainty kept visible.

This English reading guide summarises the French Benin dossier; it is not a full translation of the encyclopedia. Navigation and linked chapters remain in French.

What is documented?

The dossier separates historical archives, international statistical estimates and nationally confirmed contemporary data. FAOSTAT estimates must not be presented as field measurements or proof of a verified local supply chain.

Read the full French Benin dossier

What remains uncertain?

Local confirmation of production statistics, current operators and prices requires dated evidence. This guide does not invent a Beninese origin, an operating farm or a certification where evidence is missing.

How to check a claim

Record the source, year, unit, geographic scope and estimation flag. Production is not the same as exports, and a crop year is not necessarily a calendar year. Preserve disagreement between sources rather than combining incompatible numbers.

Reading coffee claims about Benin

This page is a reading guide, not a full translation of the French dossier. Use it to understand what a source can establish, then follow the French dossier for the detailed references.

Start with the product and the period. A figure for green coffee production is not the same measurement as exports, planted area or roasted coffee sales. A calendar year and a coffee crop year may cover different periods.

Keep the status of a figure beside the figure itself. An estimate is not a nationally confirmed measurement. A historical report can document the past without measuring the present. Do not turn missing information into zero production.

The Benin agricultural survey report for the 2024-2025 season is dated April 2026. A search of its complete text did not establish a national coffee production series. This check does not settle what may exist in other national records, so it does not remove the uncertainty attached to the estimates discussed on this site.

The two English calculators are a first selection. They calculate mass ratios; they do not reproduce every French tool or determine whether a coffee, a farm or a production estimate has been verified.
